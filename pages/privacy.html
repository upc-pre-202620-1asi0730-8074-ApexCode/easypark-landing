Privacy policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Data we collect

Name, email, vehicle plates, bookings, access movements and, if you allow it, your approximate location to show nearby parking.

2. Purpose

We use the data to manage your account, process bookings, record access, send service notifications and produce aggregated reports for operators.

3. Legal basis

Processing follows Peru's Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 29733) and its regulations.

4. Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, cancellation and objection (ARCO) by writing to contacto@easypark.pe.

5. Security

We apply technical measures such as encrypted communication (HTTPS) and token-based authentication to protect your information.

6. Local storage

This site does not use cookies or store data in your browser.
