About us

A team focused on solving an everyday problem

EasyPark is an ApexCode project that makes finding parking easier in busy urban areas and gives operators digital tools to run their lot with real-time data instead of notebooks and paper tickets.

Mission

Provide an accessible solution that makes parking management easier and reduces the time drivers spend looking for a space.

Vision

Become a leading platform for digital urban parking management, helping drivers and operators use spaces in a more organized and reliable way.

  1. 01

    Choose your parking lot

    Compare price, distance and available spaces near your destination.

  2. 02

    Schedule date and time

    Confirm your booking

  4. 04

    Arrive and park

About the product

EasyPark: less circling around, more control over your lot

EasyPark is a web platform that connects the daily operation of small and medium parking lots with the information drivers need.

  • Open network: independent parking lots from different operators in one place.
  • Reliable availability: generated by the parking lot's own operation.
  • Progressive adoption: start with digital records and add IoT sensors when you need them.
